Live Bridge privacy

Privacy disclosure

Single purpose. Livefy Live Bridge connects visible TikTok LIVE operational data to the workspace selected by the user.

Data collected

Only while capture is enabled: visible public LIVE comments and usernames, visible product names and prices, visible audience count, TikTok page host/type, extension version, connection status and device name.

Data never collected

Passwords, authentication cookies, payment information, private messages, typed form values, non-TikTok browsing history, screenshots, microphone, camera and precise location.

Use and retention

Captured data is transmitted over HTTPS to the user's Livefy workspace for live operations, analytics and user-configured automation. It is not sold, used for advertising or shared for unrelated purposes. Users can pause capture, disconnect a device and delete workspace records.

Consent and control

The extension requires an explicit consent checkbox before pairing. Capture can be disabled at any time from the extension popup. Revoking the device in Livefy immediately invalidates its credential.
